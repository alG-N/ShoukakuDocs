# Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 2026-06-03

This Privacy Policy explains what information Shoukaku ("Bot", "Service") may process and how it is used.

## 1. Information We Process

To provide functionality, the Bot may process:

- Discord User IDs
- Discord Server (Guild) IDs
- Discord Channel IDs
- Discord Role IDs
- Command usage information
- Moderation records created through Bot features
- Music queue and playback data
- Technical logs related to reliability and abuse prevention

The Bot does not collect passwords, payment information, or private account credentials.

## 2. How Information Is Used

Information is used solely to:

- Execute commands
- Provide moderation functionality
- Operate music and media features
- Prevent abuse and spam
- Improve reliability and performance
- Diagnose technical issues

## 3. Data Sharing

We do not sell user data.

Information may only be disclosed when required by law, to protect the Service, or to respond to legitimate legal requests.

## 4. Data Retention

Data is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the Bot, provide features, maintain moderation records, or protect the Service from abuse.

Certain cached or temporary data may be automatically removed.

## 5. User Requests

Users may request information regarding data associated with their Discord account or request deletion where technically and legally feasible.

## 6. Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect stored information.

However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time.

Continued use of the Bot after changes are published constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

## 8. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, please contact the Bot owner through the official support server.
