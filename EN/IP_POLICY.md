# Copyright and Intellectual Property Policy

**Effective date:** July 29, 2026

Shoukaku respects copyright, trademarks, privacy, and other intellectual-property rights. This policy explains how complaints concerning the official hosted Service are handled.

This policy is an operational notice-and-action process. It does not state that Shoukaku qualifies for any particular statutory safe harbor in any country.

## 1. Contact

Send copyright and intellectual-property notices to:

**Email:** whittylord@gmail.com  
**Subject:** `Shoukaku IP Notice`

Do not post allegedly infringing material, personal data, or confidential evidence in a public GitHub issue or Discord channel.

## 2. Information to Include

A notice should include:

1. the complainant's name and contact information;
2. identification of the protected work, mark, or other right;
3. the Discord message, command result, URL, media identifier, server ID, or other information sufficient to locate the material or integration;
4. an explanation of why the use is unauthorized or unlawful;
5. a good-faith statement that the complaint is accurate;
6. confirmation that the complainant is the rights holder or authorized to act for the rights holder;
7. a physical or electronic signature.

The Operator may request missing information before acting.

## 3. Response Process

After receiving a sufficiently detailed notice, the Operator may:

- preserve limited evidence needed to investigate;
- disable a source, command, link, cache entry, or provider;
- remove or disable access to temporary material;
- contact the affected user or server administrator where appropriate;
- ask the upstream platform or provider to act;
- reject a notice that is incomplete, abusive, fraudulent, or outside the Operator's control.

The Operator will act as promptly as reasonably possible in light of the technical and legal circumstances.

## 4. Counter-Notice or Dispute

A person affected by a removal or restriction may email a counter-notice containing:

1. identification of the affected material or feature;
2. the reason the person believes the restriction was mistaken;
3. supporting authorization, license, public-domain status, or other relevant evidence;
4. contact information and a good-faith accuracy statement;
5. a physical or electronic signature.

The Operator may keep content disabled while the parties resolve the dispute or where continued access creates legal, platform, safety, or operational risk.

## 5. Repeat Infringement and Abuse

The Operator may restrict or terminate users, servers, sources, or integrations associated with repeated credible infringement complaints, deliberate circumvention, fraudulent notices, or abuse of the complaint process.

## 6. Media Feature Restrictions

The official hosted deployment enables `/download` for supported public media. Users may request only material they own, have permission to use, or may use under applicable law. `/download` and other music or media features must not be used for private, paid, restricted, or DRM-protected content, or for redistribution without permission.

The Service does not accept user account passwords, cookies, or authentication tokens for media access.

## 7. United States DMCA Notices

A notice may contain the elements commonly required by 17 U.S.C. § 512, but this policy does not represent that the Operator has registered a designated DMCA agent or is entitled to United States safe-harbor protection. The Operator should obtain legal advice before making such a claim.
