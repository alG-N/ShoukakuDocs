# Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability

Report suspected vulnerabilities privately to **whittylord@gmail.com** with the subject:

`Shoukaku Security Report`

Include, where possible:

- the affected component or endpoint;
- reproduction steps;
- expected and actual behavior;
- potential impact;
- logs or screenshots with secrets and personal data removed;
- a safe proof of concept;
- your preferred contact details.

Do not report active vulnerabilities, tokens, credentials, personal data, private logs, or exploit instructions in public GitHub issues or public Discord channels.

## Safe Testing Rules

Good-faith testing must:

- use accounts and servers you control or have permission to test;
- avoid accessing, changing, deleting, or publishing other people's data;
- avoid denial of service, spam, social engineering, credential attacks, and persistence;
- stop when sensitive data or unauthorized access is encountered;
- give the Operator a reasonable opportunity to investigate before public disclosure.

This policy does not authorize conduct that violates law, Discord rules, contracts, or third-party rights.

## Response and Remediation

The Operator will review credible reports, may request additional details, and will prioritize issues based on severity and exploitability. Fixes may include disabling a feature, rotating secrets, blocking traffic, patching code, or notifying affected parties and Discord where required.

No reward or payment is promised unless agreed in writing before testing.

## Supported Versions

Only the current official hosted Service and the current maintained backend branch are supported. Forks and independently hosted copies must contact their own operator.

## Incident Notices

Security incidents involving personal data or Discord API data will be investigated and reported to users, Discord, providers, or authorities where required by applicable law or platform terms.
